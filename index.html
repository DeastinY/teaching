About me

Hey there! I'm a medical AI researcher based in Aachen, Germany, working with Prof. Schuppert's team at the Joint Research Center of Computational Biomedicine.

My work sits at the intersection of healthcare and technology — I build predictive models using AI and Machine Learning to help improve patient outcomes, particularly with time-series data. I'm also passionate about making High Performance Computing more accessible to researchers at German University Hospitals through the NHR4CES Simulation and Data Lab.

When I'm not diving into data, you'll probably find me hunting for the perfect cup of coffee or getting creative with photography, video, or music.
